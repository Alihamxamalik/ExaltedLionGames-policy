Untangle Glass: Line Puzzle

Privacy policy · Effective 25 November 2026

Untangle Glass: Line Puzzle is a puzzle game for Android made by Exalted Lion Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version

The game does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no accounts, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking. It goes online for one thing only: to download picture albums.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress and settings on your device only: the levels you have finished, your coins and hints, and your choices for sound, vibration and theme. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

Album downloads

The first three albums come with the game. Each later album downloads once, the first time you reach it, and then plays offline. The game only asks for the album files; it sends nothing about you. Like any web server, our file storage at Cloudflare sees your device's internet address while it sends the files. We don't keep that address or use it for anything.

Permissions

The game asks Android for two permissions: vibration, to give small taps of feedback while you play, and internet access, used only to download albums. You can turn vibration off in the game's pause menu.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party services that collect information: no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Album files are stored with Cloudflare R2. Google Play may collect information about installs and crashes under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that changes this, such as ads or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
