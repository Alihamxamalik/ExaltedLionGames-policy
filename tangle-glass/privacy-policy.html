Tangle Glass

Privacy policy · Effective 25 November 2026

Tangle Glass is a puzzle game for Android made by Exalted Lion Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version

Tangle Glass does not collect, send or share any personal information. It works without an internet connection, has no accounts, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress and settings on your device only: the levels you have finished, your coins and hints, and your choices for sound, vibration and theme. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

Permissions

The game asks Android for one permission, vibration, to give small taps of feedback while you play. You can turn this off in the game's pause menu.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party services that collect information: no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Google Play may collect information about installs and crashes under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that changes this, such as ads or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
